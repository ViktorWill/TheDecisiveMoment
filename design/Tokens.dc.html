Rules that are design decisions, not polish
Uncertainty is part of the answer. Every EV carries its ± and every recommendation is rounded to what the model supports. No 1/243.
Scores are prose, never numbers. “137 photos nearby · plaza · curated” tells you something. “0.87” does not.
Only real marks. The barrel scale renders the engraved marks of the selected lens. A distance you cannot physically set is worse than no advice.
